Product Overview

CoffeeCalendar is a one of a kind advent calendar that can be used year round! Small enough to fit anywhere in your kitchen, it provides unique coffee recipes for every week of the year. Mid-July? Try out the perfect recipe for our Salted Honey Latte. End of December? Cuddle up with our signature Gingerbread Mocha. Ditch the days of boring, bitter coffee, and add something to your routine that will actually get you out of bed and excited for the day!

Unique Features

What makes our product so special?

Reusable

Unlike many other advent calendars, ours is reusable. Each week, instead of pulling off and discarding previous pages, you simply flip it to the back, revealing the new page. The previous page is saved, so you can always go back and find your favorite recipes. This also allows you to pick up our product in any month of the year.

Easy to use

The recipes contain exact measurements and tells you precisely how to assemble the coffee. Additionally, at the beginning of each month, there is a complete list that contains everything you will need to make all the recipes for that month. This gives you one single reference of exactly what you'll need to make all the delicious coffees for that month, and makes an easy addition to your grocery list.

Themed Recipes

There is one recipe for each week of the year, and the recipe that you will find will match the season that the week is in! Our summer recipes will feature lighter drinks with herbal and cirtus flavors, while our winter recipes will feature warm, cozy drinks with cinnamon and nutmeg flavors. This way, your coffee will always be something you're craving.

Modifiable Recipes

The recipes are easily modifiable if you're someone who prefers oat milk, less sugar, or any other possible modification. The recipes give you the baseline of what to create, and will include suggestions of modifications that could be made. Additionally, each recipe has instructions for it to be made either hot or iced, so you can make the coffee exactly how you want it.
